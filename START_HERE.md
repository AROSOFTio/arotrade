# Start Here

AroPilot AI is built around direct MT5 integration. Start by connecting MetaTrader 5 through the AroPilot Expert Advisor, then use the dashboard for market intelligence, provider comparison, consensus, signals, risk controls, and journal review.

Required first action: create a Direct MT5 bridge from Broker Accounts and attach the EA to MT5.

Optional: configure additional AI providers and the MetaApi adapter only when needed.
